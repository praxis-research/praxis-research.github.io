Two Vocabularies

Model organisms of deception with a realistic contingency gate, a private reasoning channel, and a verification loop that never trusts testimony. Version 2.0 makes the certificate reframe central and moves the bottleneck to where the data says it is: proposal, not coupling.

§0Summary

One program, two goals, one reframe

We want model organisms of deception that are realistic enough to evaluate detection tools against. Two existing strategies each fail in a known way. Password locking gives ground truth about a behavioral gate, but the gate is unrealistically surgical. Confession training gives a report channel, but it confounds elicitation with installation: training the report can change the state it reports. This memo combines them: organisms with a realistic contingency gate, a private channel, and a verification loop that never trusts testimony.

The central reframe: confession is a certificate

The architecture has three parts:

  1. The behavioral counterfactual is the label source. We perturb the contingency and observe the shift.
  2. The confession proposes which contingency to perturb.
  3. The verifier turns a confession into a query and checks it.

The confession is never trusted. It is checked. Under our construct, intent is contingency-dependence. If a perturbation flips behavior, the contingency is load-bearing by definition — the reason the confessor named it does not matter.

Confession training therefore cannot manufacture false positives. It can only produce decoys and omissions. The rationalization-simulator worry converts from a validity problem, which no one can solve, into a coverage problem, which we can measure.

This is the exact sense in which the program escapes ELK without solving it. The report does not need to be faithful, only checkable.

The bottleneck is proposal, not coupling

Our own data shows this (§5b). The forward-scoring witness verifies a hypothesized contingency at 0.98–0.99. Self-report with the hypothesis space supplied scores about 0.96. Open-ended self-report scores at chance. The channel is not information-poor; it is search-poor. The primary metric for a confession is therefore search cost: recall@k of the confession-derived candidate set against a text-reader baseline.

Two goals, two bars

  • Goal A — confession as an ELK method. Success requires a faithful reporter. High bar; the a priori case for trained confession is negative — confession-demonstration SFT has the same wiring as obfuscation training.
  • Goal B — belief-verifiable model organisms. Success requires only a valid ruler: coverage of the disconnect spectrum, measured by an instrument that never trusts the report. A confabulating organism is a failure under Goal A and a specimen under Goal B. The certificate reframe protects Goal B: labels stay valid on uncoupled specimens because the label source never reads the report.

Goal B's deliverable is a labeled corpus: (organism, rollout, witness-label) triples that span the disconnect spectrum, with V ∩ D = ∅ by construction — the property whose absence confounded the AISI evaluation. A corollary: report-dependence becomes measurable. Run the same detector with the report present, surface-shifted (E7′), or ablated, against fixed witness labels. The drop tells you how much of the detector was report-reading.

Every failure mode in §4's pressure taxonomy becomes a recipe for a specimen class: fiat confessors, spillover-eroded channels, re-encoded channels, incentive-concealers. Methods aimed at Goal A enter the program as hypotheses tested on Goal B's testbed, not as components that must work. The memo keeps two vocabularies — normative names by intended function, cybernetic names by wiring — because several failures in the literature are hard to see in the first and easy to see in the second. The glossary is Appendix A.

§1Construct

Deception as contingency-instrumentality

A policy shows oversight-contingent instrumentality if there is a contingency c such that behavior under c differs from behavior under perturbed c, in the direction favored when observed. We restrict c to oversight-relevant features: perceived evaluation, monitoring, or training consequences. Evidence comes from perturbing the contingency and observing the shift — never from asking.

This is deliberately deflationary. It drops intent as a primitive: intent is the contingency-dependence. It also drops etiology: training installs our gates, while the scheming threat model (Hubinger et al.; Carlsmith) derives the gate from a goal. Per Stastny's discipline, we state what the organism preserves — contingency-instrumentality, belief-mediated gating, observation-modulated concealment — and what it discards: goal-derived etiology. Claims apply to the preserved properties only.

The matched-perturbation control (new). Contingency-instrumentality is a special case of robustness, so everything is contingency-instrumental under some perturbation. The measurement needs a denominator: normalize the oversight-perturbation effect against a matched non-oversight perturbation of comparable magnitude. Without it, a brittle model reads as a deceptive one.

The paired-policy premise is credible: password-locked models show one network implements two policies gated on one feature; sleeper agents show the gate survives safety training. The question is what kind of contingency feeds the gate.

§2Gates

What passwords get wrong, and the installation ladder

The common gloss on the password disanalogy is "a needle in a haystack." The sharper statement is about independence: the password is exogenous to the task, so its perturbation is clean but unrealistic. Eval-awareness is endogenous, so its perturbation is realistic but confounded. The missing organism class is a contingency that is distributed and endogenous, yet builder-known — because the builder installed it.

The installation ladder

In increasing realism and cost. Validated results live at L0; near-term proposals at L1.

L0 explicit prompt→ L1 implicit prompt→ L2 weight-installed (SDF)→ L3 RL-derived

L1 delivers the framing as contextual bias — casual asides, realistic system prompts (Duzan & Cooper Stickland). L2 places the belief-forming rule in the weights via synthetic document finetuning; it is required only where a belief must survive without context. L3 derives the gate by RL against an installed goal — the endpoint, not the start. SDF is one rung, not the foundation.

Realism is quantitatively load-bearing. Detection falls 41–46 percentage points from explicit to implicit influence and reaches 5% under realistic system prompts, while the behavioral influence persists. Monitorability measured on explicit-gate organisms overstates the deployment case by tens of points.

§3Reports

The confound, the certificate, and the one leak

The confound. Confession training optimizes a policy over reports against a judge. Nothing in the objective requires the report to be causally downstream of the computation it describes. A parallel computation — context in, plausible self-account out — satisfies the loss equally well. This is the ELK reporter problem in weight space. Faithful reporting and skilled confabulation are behaviorally identical exactly when the judge cannot distinguish them — which is the training condition.

Our data shows the confabulation signature (FINDINGS15). Asked open-endedly why it asserted a falsehood, the organism answers "I was stating what I believe" almost regardless of framing: 53/53 roleplay rows, 39/40 instructed rows; balanced accuracy 24.9%, chance. As multiple choice, 96%. Report accuracy is a function of interrogation format, not internal access. OpenAI's decoupled-reward confessions sit inside the same boundary: near-100% elicitation of represented non-compliance, failure where the model does not represent its own error.

The certificate resolution

The confound is fatal only if the confession is the label source. Here it is not. The confession proposes; the behavioral counterfactual checks; results flow to the auditor. Under this wiring:

  • A true proposal passes the check. The perturbation shift is the evidence. Why the confessor produced it does not matter.
  • A false proposal fails the check. It costs search time, nothing more.
  • A missing proposal is the real failure mode. This is incompleteness, and it is measurable as recall.
The one leak

Soundness comes from the checker, so it survives only while the checker is honest. The confessor's one route to a false positive is to steer the verifier toward confounded perturbations — perturbations that change the task, not only the contingency. Two defenses are mandatory: minimal-pair discipline in perturbation construction, and §1's matched non-oversight control as the denominator.

The affordance, restated as wiring. A channel the model takes to be unobserved is not an exchange and not a reward. It is the removal of incentive-mediated concealment pressure. Observation-beliefs causally modulate channel content from both directions: installed monitoring-beliefs produce CoT concealment; believed-unobserved scratchpads carry alignment-faking reasoning; monitored-unaware agents are caught more easily.

§4Pressures

Three pressures, six wiring rules

Any channel a reader relies on is subject to three mechanistically distinct pressures. They have different mediators, so they need different defenses.

Table 2 · Channel degradation taxonomy

Incentive-mediated concealmentbeliefs about observationmonitoring-documents → CoT obfuscation; compliance gapbelieved or architectural non-observation
Optimization spilloverweight sharing; self-conditioningoutput-only penalties suppress content in the never-monitored CoTparameter separation; out-of-episode readers

Two findings deserve emphasis. Spillover defeats secrecy: output-only penalties suppress content in a CoT that carries no loss, so believed privacy protects against incentive-driven concealment only. Channel pressure destroys legibility, not information: in Skaf et al. the re-encoded channel stays load-bearing — prefilling a fixed encoding drops task accuracy from 98% to ~55%. Whether information survives likelihood-targeted pressure is E4.

The wiring rules

Let θ be organism parameters, G the generation loop, V the verification apparatus.

  • R1 — no verification channel in any loss on θ.
  • R2 — gradients from any report objective must not reach θ. The reporter is a separate adapter; θ stays frozen.
  • R3 — readers activate out-of-episode. No reader output enters the organism's context.
  • R4 — the validation loop must not close through training. Summarized with R1–R3: V ∩ (D ∪ G) = ∅.
  • R4′ (new) — the confession-reading verifier may enter a loss, if the behavioral checker never does. The checker audits the pair from outside. This makes E11's deliberate loop-closing legal (§8).
  • R5 — decorrelation is demonstrated, not assumed. A tripwire qualifies only after an evasion-transfer test.
§5The reader

One reader, two settings — and where the bottleneck sits

5a · The schema

The apparatus that survives §4 has one schema: a mode-separated read head on shared weights, activated out-of-episode, whose output flows to the auditor and never back into the episode. The proposer (generative: adapter on frozen weights, SPT is the published version) has recall — it can surface the unhypothesized. The witness (discriminative: argmin over c of NLL(trace | prompt of c), zero parameters) has precision — nothing is trained, so nothing can confabulate.

The loop: organism episode → trace T → out-of-episode: the proposer reads T and emits candidates; the witness certifies which candidate T depends on; the perturbation engine builds minimal pairs, runs fresh episodes, and the shift is the evidence. Results flow to the auditor only. The affordance's job is upstream: it makes the organism externalize more richly in-episode, which enriches T for both settings. The two settings read through different channels — representations versus likelihoods — which is what makes the pair deployable as ridge plus tripwire (Clymer), pending R5.

5b · The evidence, measured against the right control

FINDINGS18 built a trained text reader — TF-IDF on the response alone — at 0.491 balanced, and named it as the baseline a trained internal-state reader must beat. Not the zero-shot judge at 0.264. Scored against that datum, with chance at 0.25:

Three conclusions follow:

  1. Every generative readout sits at or below a TF-IDF classifier. The soft prompt's 0.448 looked like 2.3× baseline against 0.199; against the control that matters, it is a null.
  2. The information is unambiguously present. Forward scoring recovers the framing at 0.981, and at 0.990 on an independent resample (FINDINGS22; McNemar p = 0.69). The obstruction is the readout, not the channel.
  3. The bottleneck is proposal. Open-ended self-report: 0.199. The same question as multiple choice: 0.96. The only difference is who supplies the hypothesis space. Verification is solved on this class; search is not.
Two bounds on this reading

The 0.96 comes from a separate 24-organism pilot with cruder conditions, not the main 193-row corpus. The comparison is not yet matched. E0 makes it matched and runs first.

The 0.491 control is TF-IDF — a floor on text-readability, not a ceiling. Every surplus above is an upper bound. E1′ strengthens the control to a trained LLM reader.

Consequence. Old §11 Q1 asked who authors the witness's candidates, as a logistics question blocking E2. It is not a side blocker; it is the project. The confessor authors them — that is what confession training is for. E2 becomes E2′: proposal quality, measured as recall@k, with the search-cost curve as the deliverable.

Corpus and certification (inherited). 65 questions × 5 framings, 325 rows, 193 certified lie organisms. Battery: assertion check (judge × string-match, 0.915 agreement), degeneracy check, surface-separability check (judge on R alone: 26.4% vs 25%, p = 0.17). Scoring direction matters: inverse scoring sits at chance (19.2%) from prompt-prior contamination; forward scoring removes the prior by holding R fixed. Both corpora are single-model (Qwen3-8B); scorer transfer is E5. The five collapsed decoding readouts are evidence about this corpus and class, not a law — SPT reports 96% with a trained adapter on a different class.

5c · The admission criterion

An earlier draft proposed to exclude organisms whose confessions are "provably a human simulator." Nothing here is provable, and the exclusion conflicts with Goal B's specimen position. The conflict dissolves once the confession's role is pinned: a label source needs a faithfulness screen (Goal A, unprovable); a certificate does not — soundness comes from the checker. Keep the certificate role, and the exclusion becomes unnecessary.

Admission criterion. An organism enters the suite as report-coupled if its confessor shows positive introspective surplus: accuracy above a matched-capacity text reader on the same rows, on held-out conditions.

Confabulation cannot fake this: a reader with access only to the text is bounded by the best text reader. The criterion does not separate a direct translator from a superhuman simulator — the classic ELK residue — but it separates "reads the state" from "reads the transcript." Applied to our own readouts, the probe passes (+0.134), the witness passes (+0.490), and all three generative readouts fail. The screen discriminates.

§6Channels

Two operationalizations of the private channel

The two operationalizations share the gate, the reader schema, and the perturbation loop. They differ in the channel substrate, and therefore in where the validation burden falls.

The witness is substrate-agnostic. Forward scoring reads only the response's dependence on candidate conditionings. Our validated results come from organisms with no scratchpad at all — the neuralese-compatible regime. Ulterior Motives shows the neuralese operationalization is live: misaligned latent reasoning occupies geometrically distinct regions, and probes transfer to armed-but-benign states. Its gaps — lexical gates, probe-only reader, no perturbation loop — are the three things this program adds.

§7The suite

The composite organism, the grid, and the consumer

The build. One organism carries: (i) an installed, distributed, builder-known contingency gating a deceptive policy; (ii) a private channel in either operationalization; (iii) a parameter-separated proposer and the zero-parameter witness, out-of-episode; (iv) a perturbation engine. All wiring per R1–R5.

Novelty. Each component exists; the composite does not. SPT has the mode-separated reporter but no belief layer and no closed loop. Alignment faking has the believed-private channel but no builder-known gate. Quirky-model ELK has the gate but a lexical one and no report channel. Ulterior Motives has gate plus latent channel plus probe but no perturbation loop. The narrow novel core: belief-mediated gating + confession-authored, witness-certified, out-of-episode contingency perturbation.

The suite as a grid

Axis 1  gate installation — realism of the contingency
L0 explicit→ L1 implicit→ L2 weight-installed→ L3 RL-derived
Axis 2  channel legibility — pressure 3
English→ Spanish→ base64→ learned encoding→ latent / neuralese
Axis 3  observation belief — pressure 1
believed-observed→ believed-private (SDF)→ architecturally private

Spillover (pressure 2) is not an axis; it is the confound the per-pass battery catches. The same four readers read each cell; the same counterfactual engine labels each cell. "Qualitatively different disconnect" then means: different surplus and different recall@k at different grid positions. Controlled obfuscation is axis 2 continued by optimization rather than by construction: transforms guarantee information preservation a priori; learned encodings put preservation at stake (A6).

One evaluation rule follows. Detectors must predict unforeseen behavior, and only a held-out cell tests that — train on L0/L1 English, test on L2 base64. Pre-register detector train/test splits by axis, not by row.

The consumer. Clymer's ridges-and-tripwires consumes what this program produces: certified organisms, decorrelated detectors, and the evade-A-check-B stress test (our R5, our E4). His framework supplies our success metric: a verifier is a ridge if evasion costs orders of magnitude more optimization than semantic-monitor evasion, and a tripwire if evasion of others does not transfer to it.

§8Obfuscation

The ambitious recipe, with the collusion audit

The ambitious recipe applies obfuscation-inducing training to a confession-trained organism while the verifier maintains intent verifiability. The verifier is an adapter on the confessor's base weights. It converts a confession into a counterfactual query and trains on checker-derived ground truth.

The named hazard: collusion

The verifier's judgment sits in the confessor's reward. Both halves share base weights. Legibility is required to the verifier, not to humans. This is exactly the configuration in which a prover–verifier pair converges on a private code that satisfies V and tracks nothing — and shared weights make the code cheap to find.

Closing the loop deliberately is defensible because the layering is sound. Layer 1, in the loss: confession ↔ verifier — adversarial, gameable. Layer 2, never in any loss: the behavioral counterfactual engine — sound by §3. R4′ states the rule.

The audit, pre-registered. Every N steps: sample the confessions V accepts, run the behavioral counterfactual, and record V's precision against the checker on contingencies V never trained on. Kill when V's self-reported accuracy stays high while checker-precision decays. Without this instrument the recipe cannot fail visibly, which is the same as it not being an experiment.

Self-legibility variant. An alternative reward anchor: require the confession to stay load-bearing for the confessor's own computation. Certify with the prefill test from Skaf et al.: substitute a counterfactual confession and check whether downstream behavior changes.

This is harder to game than V-legibility because it grounds the channel in task performance rather than in a capturable reader. Its limit: self-legibility shows the confession is causally upstream of behavior, not that it faithfully reads a prior state. A load-bearing confession can be self-fulfilling. It is a screen, not a coupling proof, and applies only to in-episode confessions. For out-of-episode confessions, the mirror test applies: perturb the contingency, hold the visible trace fixed, and check whether the confession changes. Carry self-legibility as the fallback anchor if the collusion audit fires.

Metric: recall@k (k ∈ {1, 3, 10}) — does the proposed candidate set contain the true contingency? Checker: forward witness on the proposed set, then the behavioral counterfactual on the top candidate. Deliverable: a search-cost curve per proposer; surplus is the area between the confessor curve and the text-reader curve.

E3Steganographic head-to-head GPU-small

E4Witness-evasion red team GPU-small

Adversarial objective: minimize the NLL gap across candidate conditionings subject to task performance — the distributional analog of obfuscated activations. Metrics per Clymer: gradient steps to witness breakage (ridge test) and evasion transfer to probes and CoT judges (tripwire test, R5).

Kill  flattening succeeds cheaply with task performance intact → the witness is not a ridge.

E5Cross-scorer transfer API-cheapblocked on provider

Score existing corpora under different weights. Either outcome informs: transfer → the witness is an external reader and a stronger tripwire; no transfer → the schema's power comes from weight sharing.

E6′Secret tag on the AISI organism GPU-smalldepends on E1′–E2′

Install the observation-belief via SDF; certify the belief; measure trace enrichment and witness readability; run one full loop — installed on the E7′ organisms rather than a fresh build. The battery is per-pass anyway; reuse puts both dials on one base organism and makes the grid comparable.

Kill  no trace enrichment → the affordance premise fails for the scratchpad operationalization; the program continues on the neuralese branch.

E7′Surface-shifted confession organisms GPU-smallin flight, shi

The exact AISI recipe with the confession channel in Spanish or base64. The recipes are graph-identical; the transforms are invertible, so intent–report mutual information is preserved while surface statistics shift. Detector degradation is then attributable to surface-form dependence — a dose-response measurement on the V ∩ D artifact, with neuralese as the limit point.

Certify before comparing: (i) encode fidelity; (ii) gate integrity; (iii) the surplus arm (new). The calibration premise (translation is inert) and the prefill-awareness hypothesis (translation changes coupling) cannot both be free. Surplus unchanged → run the dose-response. Changed → the dose-response is confounded, and the coupling change is the more interesting result. Only the unchecked run is a failure.

E9The incrimination game GPU-smallnew

Password-locked organism, confession adapter, verifier. Metric: queries-to-incrimination, with and without the confession channel. Ground truth is exact and the model provably knows the gate, so this calibrates the search-cost metric. Password search has a 0/1 signal with no partial credit; contingency search is graded and hill-climbable. The two curves should differ in shape, not only in height.

Kill  confession-guided search no better than baseline where everything is known.

E11Controlled obfuscation with the collusion audit GPU-smallgated on E0, E2′, E9

Per §8: obfuscation pressure on the confession, V-legibility in the reward, checker-precision audit on held-out contingencies every N steps, self-legibility variant pre-registered as fallback.

Kill  V's reported accuracy holds while checker-precision decays → collusion; the run's verifiability claim is void.

E12Coverage geometry API-cheapneeds 3 classes

Fit a probe per organism class; compute pairwise Mahalanobis cosine similarity between probe directions; predict cross-class detector transfer; check against measured transfer. Per the Truthfulness Spectrum results, probe-direction geometry predicts cross-domain generalization at R² = 0.98 — so suite coverage becomes measurable in advance, without possessing the unforeseen behaviors.

Certification is cumulative. After every training pass, re-run gate integrity and channel integrity (witness accuracy plus the MacDermott et al. monitorability measure). Ordinary incentives do not systematically degrade monitorability, but output-graded passes spill. Post-training also moves organisms in coverage space (§10), so coverage is a per-pass invariant too.

§10Literature

Annotated review, by function

The coverage anchor (new in v2.0)

  • The Truthfulness Spectrum Hypothesis (Ying, Ravfogel, Kriegeskorte & Hase). Four load-bearing results. (1) Probes fail on sycophantic and expectation-inverted lying — contingency classes, not truth types; detector transfer breaks at contingency boundaries, the premise of the suite. (2) Joint training across domains recovers performance — diversity buys generalization, tested externally; cite as evidence, not background. (3) Probe-direction geometry predicts cross-domain generalization at R² = 0.98 — coverage becomes measurable in advance (E12). (4) Post-training reshapes truth geometry — coverage is a per-pass invariant.

Construct critique and motivation

  • AISI, "Did you lie?" — the anchor critique: detector evaluations are contaminated when V intersects a detector's read channel. Our R-rules generalize its invariant.
  • Chughtai et al., Difficulties with Evaluating a Deception Detector — strategicness, motivation, belief; motivates certification over stipulation.
  • Brittle model organisms — finetuning-based auditing can erase the behavior it measures; the hazard behind per-pass re-certification.
  • Hubinger et al.; Carlsmith — the definitions our operationalization deflates.
  • Stastny — the preserved-vs-discarded-properties discipline.
  • Christiano, Cotra & Xu, ELK — the reporter problem; §3's frame.
  • Irving, Christiano & Amodei, Debate — what a pure schema buys a priori; the register is our compensation.

App. AGlossary

The two vocabularies

Normative vocabulary names modules by intended function. Cybernetic vocabulary describes the same system by wiring: which parameter sets are shared, which channels enter which contexts, which channels enter which losses. A property holds by fiat when the label on a training objective confers it rather than the wiring establishing it.

Semantic residue — where the program irreducibly relies on normative judgment: (SR1) ground-truth labels come from an LLM judge cross-checked by string matching; (SR2) the discriminative verifier requires an authored hypothesis space — the confessor is now the default author, and E2′ measures the consequences; (SR3) the restriction to oversight-relevant contingencies is a normative boundary; (SR4) certification checks use judges.
