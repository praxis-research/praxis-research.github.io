We do alignment research to ensure human oversight and control over *future* AIs.

The motivation for our work is explained in [this talk](https://www.figma.com/deck/qgKa4YDIF9a6LF83AwdoPv/Fall-2025?node-id=19-114).

The best way to get in touch is by using our [contact form](https://airtable.com/appHnvwbow1epprSd/pagqZfMyMvha6rWnD/form). We invite all researchers to collaborate on open problems through [Sprints](/sprints/).

## Our current focuses are

### Deception

- [Language Models Learn to Mislead Humans via RLHF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12822)
- [Adaptive Deployment of Untrusted LLMs Reduces Distributed Threats](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17693)

### Collusion

- [LLM Evaluators Recognize and Favor Their Own Generations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13076)
- [Spontaneous Reward Hacking in Iterative Self-Refinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04549)

### Honesty

- [Unsupervised Elicitation of Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10139)
- [Self-Improvement as Coherence Optimization](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2601.13566)
