This is a list of mini-projects relevant to our core research, and invite
researchers of all background to collaborate with us on them. When designing
these projects, we prioritize:

- **Fun.** They are based on papers that we are most excited about.
- **Learning.** New researchers can use them to gain hands-on experience.
- **Relevance.** They are often sub-problems of projects we actively pursue.
- **Scope.** Each should take less than a week.

## Process

Our goal is to foster new collaborations with a simple process:

1. Pick a project, follow instructions in the document.
2. Submit your work (code and report) via [this form](https://airtable.com/appHnvwbow1epprSd/pagaKp5UPAr4jLIxo/form).
3. If selected, we will invite you to a second phase to explore more open-ended
   questions with support of computational resources and mentorship.
